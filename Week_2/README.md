# Week 2 - Advanced Data Visualization and Storytelling with Python

## Objective

The objective of Week 2 was to develop advanced visualizations and communicate employee attrition patterns through effective data storytelling.

## Dataset

IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance Dataset

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Visualizations

1. Attrition Rate by Age Group
2. Attrition Rate by Job Role
3. Monthly Income by Job Level and Attrition
4. Satisfaction Factors and Attrition
5. Overtime and Job Level vs Attrition

## Analysis Areas

- Employee age and attrition
- Job role and attrition
- Monthly income and job level
- Employee satisfaction and work-life balance
- Overtime and job level
